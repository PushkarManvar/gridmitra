Microgrid Assets

Standardized multi-vector generation and energy storage components.

solar_power

SOLAR PHOTOVOLTAIC

Zero marginal fuel cost

Primary renewable source during daylight hours. Yield calculated via GHI irradiance curves, panel temperature coefficients, and inverter clipping curves.

Priority: Must-Take Curtailment Penalty: $0.02/kWh
mode_fan

WIND TURBINE GENERATION

Stochastic intermittent profile

Optional renewable generation with hourly availability. Utilizes power cut-in/cut-out curves (3.5 m/s to 25 m/s) with aerodynamic wake mitigation factors.

Priority: Co-Renewable Ramp Constraints: Active
battery_charging_full

BATTERY STORAGE (BESS)

LiFePO4 / Flow arbitrage

Stores renewable surplus and supplies energy during later demand periods while respecting reserve state-of-charge (min 20% SoC) and C-rate charge/discharge limits.

Round-Trip Eff: 89.5% Degradation Model: Enabled
oil_barrel

DIESEL / THERMAL GENSET

Firm dispatchable backup

Firm backup generation engaged strictly when renewable supply and permitted battery discharge cannot satisfy demand or reserve constraints. High fuel/emissions cost.

Min Run-Time: 60 mins Carbon Factor: 2.68 kg/L

What is Renewable Curtailment?

Thermal & Inverter Constraint

Curtailment occurs when generated solar or wind output exceeds immediate community demand AND the battery storage system is unable to absorb additional power (either fully charged or at maximum C-rate charging current). To maintain 50/60Hz grid stability, inverters throttle generation.
