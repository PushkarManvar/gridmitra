build Quick Troubleshooting

6 operational scenarios
1

Optimization will not start

Suggested checks: Validate the 24-hour dataset. Check battery reserve and capacity limits. Verify diesel generator values. Confirm all P1–P4 loads are assigned.

2

Forecast data is unavailable

GridMitra can automatically switch to the prepared dataset when the live weather source is unavailable or timing out.

3

Emergency Plan generated

This is not a solver failure. Total physical supply was insufficient to serve all forecast demand, so GridMitra generated the best feasible priority-aware dispatch.

4

Optimization failed

A technical or solver problem prevented the run from completing. Usually caused by infeasible manual constraint overrides or numerical divergence.

5

CSV upload rejected

Checks: 24 hourly records required • No negative values • Valid time sequence • Required demand columns present (kW, solar_irradiance, wind_mps).

6

Renewable energy was curtailed

Renewable generation can be curtailed when demand is already satisfied and battery charging capacity is unavailable (SoC maxed or C-rate limit hit).

quiz Frequently Asked Questions

Dispatcher Handbook

Why did GridMitra activate diesel? 01

Diesel is used when renewable generation and permitted battery discharge cannot satisfy forecast demand.

Why did the battery not discharge? 02

Battery discharge may be limited to preserve the configured reserve for upcoming critical demand or to avoid exceeding maximum hourly inverter C-rate discharge thresholds.

Why was P4 demand reduced? 03

Flexible P4 demand is reduced before higher-priority services during a supply shortage to defend Tier 1 lifeline loads.

Can P1 demand ever be reduced? 04

Yes, but only when total available physical supply is insufficient even after lower-priority demand (P2, P3, P4) has been reduced to zero.

Why is renewable energy curtailed? 05

Available renewable generation may exceed current demand and battery charging capacity. The grid cannot absorb excess energy without battery headroom.

What is an Emergency Plan? 06

A successfully solved dispatch where some demand remains unserved because physical supply is insufficient to satisfy total demand without violating physical constraints.

Does GridMitra directly control the microgrid? 07

No. GridMitra provides dispatch recommendations for operator review. Human operators maintain operational authority.

Are explanations generated using AI? 08

GridMitra’s MVP explanations are rule-generated from optimization outputs and active constraints, guaranteeing deterministic and auditable operational traces.
